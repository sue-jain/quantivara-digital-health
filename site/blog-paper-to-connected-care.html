FROM PAPER TO POSSIBILITY

A patient arrives at reception carrying a prescription from their last visit. It is a small piece of paper, but it holds useful context: what was discussed, what was prescribed, and what comes next.

For us, that moment is a useful starting point for designing connected care. How can technology help preserve that context while fitting into the way a practice already works?

Start with what is already there.

A paper-friendly workflow begins with capture. A receptionist or clinician can photograph the document with a phone, then review the information before adding it to the patient’s record. The original document remains part of the story, rather than disappearing behind a summary.

Make review part of the routine.

Capturing a document is only the first step. Any information extracted by AI needs a human review. Names, dates, and clinical details deserve a careful check, and the team should be able to return to the source whenever something is unclear.

The goal is simple: let a familiar moment become the beginning of a more connected record.

Build continuity, one visit at a time.

Each reviewed prescription, report, or consultation note can add another piece of context. With appropriate consent and access, the record becomes easier to carry forward into the next encounter.

This is the idea behind Santhica: start with the tools people already use, keep clinicians in control, and make room for the patient’s whole story.
