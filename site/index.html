THE CLINICAL SAFETY NET

An abnormal result
shouldn’t wait to be Escalated.

Because the record is structured rather than free text, these checks can run at the moment of review! while the patient is still in the room.

Critical lab values are parsed against reference ranges, and every new prescription is checked against the allergies and medication history already held for that patient. Each flag is raised at the point of review and has to be acknowledged, overridden or acted on before review closes.

Follow-up doesn’t stop at the alert.

Patients with pending or abnormal investigations stay on a tracked list until the result is reviewed, the action is recorded and the patient has been followed up.

A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Want to talk through your specific workflow?

Let’s have a conversation
Do I have to stop using paper prescriptions?

No. Santhica is designed to work with handwritten prescriptions. Capture a photo, review the extracted information, and add it to the patient record.

What can I do without an internet connection?

You can capture visits and work with records available on your device. Data syncs when connectivity returns. Cloud-based AI processing and sharing need a connection; we can walk through the exact workflow for your practice in a demo.

Does AI make clinical decisions for me?

No. Santhica assists with structuring information and surfacing relevant context. Clinicians review AI-generated content and remain responsible for clinical decisions.

Is Santhica ABDM certified?

ABDM certification is on our roadmap. The platform is designed around patient identity, consent, and FHIR / ABDM interoperability. We’ll discuss current integration availability for your organisation during a demo.

How do I get started?

Request a walkthrough on our contact page. Tell us about your clinic or organisation, and we’ll help you explore the relevant workflows, availability, onboarding, and pricing.
